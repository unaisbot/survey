ABOUT KIPS

A coordinated survey
across Kerala.

Kerala Invasive Plant Survey (KIPS) is designed to create a systematic, georeferenced record of invasive plant occurrence across the state through standardised field observations.

01
⌖

Systematic sampling

A statewide 2 km × 2 km grid provides a consistent spatial framework for field data collection.

02
♧

Field observations

Surveyors record invasive plants, habitat information, abundance and georeferenced quadrat observations.

03
✓

Verified records

Submitted observations can be reviewed and validated before appearing in the public survey dashboard.
